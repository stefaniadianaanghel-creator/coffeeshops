The Problem

What keeps F&B founders
up at night

01

No outlet clarity

You have 4 locations but no idea which one is profitable and which one is quietly draining the others.

02

Cash flow blind spots

Revenue looks fine on paper but the bank account is empty. The timing gap between sales and cash is invisible.

03

Food costs out of control

No one is tracking COGS as a percentage of revenue per outlet. Margins are eroding and you find out too late.

04

Expansion without data

You're opening a new location based on gut feeling, with no break-even analysis or feasibility modeling behind it.

05

Year-end surprises

You only find out how the year went when your accountant files. By then, it's too late to do anything about it.

06

No financial structure

Piles of invoices, one shared bank account, no consolidated P&L. You're running a serious business without a financial backbone.
